Straight answers.

What does an AGV or AMR cost in India?

Entry-level robots typically start around ₹20 to 30 lakh. Heavier or fully integrated fleets cost more. Payback in Indian plants typically lands between 18 and 24 months. We give you your own number on the first call.

Will it stop my production?

No. There are no floor markers and no civil work, so installation does not shut a line. Most plants start with one cell and scale only after it works.

How heavy a load can your AGVs and AMRs handle?

Standard CARRY AMRs carry 250 kg to 2,000 kg, forklift AMRs pick and stack pallets, and the tugger attachment tows full trolley trains. For heavier loads we build custom AGVs, already delivered for 10 tonnes, and can engineer higher for your requirement.

Will it work with our WMS, MES and existing AGVs?

Yes. Novus fleet software connects to WMS, MES and ERP. It follows the VDA 5050 standard, so your existing AGVs and other robots can join the same fleet.

Is it safe around my people?

Yes. Every robot has 3D LiDAR, 360-degree safety scanners, alerts, emergency stops and speed zones. It slows down near people and stops before contact.
